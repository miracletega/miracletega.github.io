05/ Content planning

Campus Thrift Pop-UpOne-Week Content Plan

Concept project
Objective
Build anticipation for a one-day pop-up and bring people through the door on launch day.
Approach
Move from mystery to detail across the week: tease, show the team, involve the audience, share the details, then go live.
  1. MondayPost

    Teaser: “Something is coming to campus.” No other details.

  2. TuesdayReel

    The team sorting and steaming the pieces.

  3. WednesdayStory

    Poll: which jacket should go on the front rack?

  4. ThursdayStory

    Location, time and a countdown sticker.

  5. FridayStories

    Pop-up day. Live stories every two hours, and reposts of customer finds.

About
